# Sentasity

[Sentasity](https://sentasity.com) finds waste and security risk across your AWS accounts. This plugin connects your assistant to Sentasity's hosted MCP server, so you can ask about your spend, your savings opportunities, your scan results and your Reserved Instance and Savings Plan recommendations in plain language. MSPs can also manage their customers' setup.

It also includes the **business-setup** skill: a short conversation about what your business does, its industry and the compliance frameworks it answers to. Sentasity uses that context to make its findings fit your business. Every change is shown to you as a preview, and nothing is saved until you approve it.

You sign in with your Sentasity account the first time the assistant uses the connector. What the assistant can see and do follows your Sentasity role, and you can limit it further when you connect.

Install in Claude Code:

```bash
/plugin install sentasity --marketplace sentasity/agent-skills
```
